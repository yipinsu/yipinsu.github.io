About

中文

苏益品，浣江实验室研究员，浙江大学平台“百人计划”研究员，绍兴大学研究员，国家级高层次青年人才、浙江省杰出青年基金获得者。2011年本科毕业于武汉大学工程力学专业，2016年博士毕业于浙江大学固体力学专业，随后赴爱尔兰、以色列、美国、意大利等开展研究工作。主要从事智能柔性材料与结构、多场耦合力学及智能传感器研究。曾入选爱尔兰、意大利国家博士后资助项目、绍兴市“名士之乡”人才计划及中国力学学会青年人才“蓄水池”计划。现任《Mechanics of Advanced Materials and Structures》编委，《Soft Science》《应用数学和力学》青年编委。

ENGLISH

Welcome to the SU Research Group. Yipin Su is a Researcher at Huanjiang Laboratory, a “Platform 100 Talents” Researcher at Zhejiang University, and a Researcher at Shaoxing University. He has been selected for a national-level high-level young talent program and is a recipient of the Zhejiang Provincial Distinguished Young Scholars Program. He received his B.Eng. in Engineering Mechanics from Wuhan University in 2011 and his Ph.D. in Solid Mechanics from Zhejiang University in 2016. He subsequently conducted research in Ireland, Israel, the United States, and Italy. His research focuses on smart soft materials and structures, multiphysics mechanics, and smart sensors. He has received postdoctoral and research support from Ireland and Italy, and has been selected for the Shaoxing “Hometown of Celebrities” Talent Program and the Young Talent Reservoir Program of the Chinese Society of Theoretical and Applied Mechanics. He currently serves on the Editorial Board of Mechanics of Advanced Materials and Structures and on the Young Editorial Boards of Soft Science and Applied Mathematics and Mechanics.
