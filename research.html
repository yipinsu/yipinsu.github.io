Research

Mechanics of smart soft materials and structures across multiple physical fields.

01

Multiphysics Mechanics of Smart Soft Materials

Constitutive modeling, finite deformation and incremental mechanics of dielectric elastomers, magneto-active hydrogels, and fiber-reinforced electroactive materials.

02

Instability and Pattern Formation

Wrinkling, buckling, snap-through, bifurcation, post-buckling and competing instability modes.

03

Waves and Vibrations

Wave propagation and vibration in finitely deformed electroactive and smart structures.

04

Smart Sensors and Devices

Mechanics-guided design of soft actuators, flexible sensors and structural health monitoring concepts.
